Paper Assembly Line

Demo

Your papers, on an assembly line.

Each paper sits in the step it’s at, from first idea to submitted. One glance shows what’s moving and what’s next.

  • Add a paper with + at the bottom of a column.
  • Drag it to the next column as it moves forward.
  • Click it for a colour, a cover image, and notes.
  • Top of a column is what you work on next.

This demo is saved only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
